# Renamer case-study design decisions

Scope: Smart Image Renamer case study and its homepage description. The user's instruction to enact the plan supersedes its older planning-only stopping point.

## Directions considered

1. Editorial notebook (Wired): a narrow reading column with large evidence figures. Strong for the chronology, but a serif magazine identity would pull further away from the rest of the portfolio.
2. Artifact-led review (Mobbin + restrained Superhuman): a compact project summary, large interface capture, predictable chapter order, and short decision/annotation pairs. Selected because it makes role, status and work quick to scan while supporting a deeper read.

## Selected system

Canvas #fafbf9; ink #252825; muted text #596159; pale sage #e9eee4; action green #47643d; separators #dce1d8. Segoe UI uses the practical Windows-tool context and requires no external font download. Left-aligned 1180px frame; split heading/prose chapters; single-column phone layout. The actual interface supplies the strongest color. No automatic motion, decorative metrics, generic icons, or gradient sections.

Mobbin informed image presentation and quiet surfaces. Wired informed reading rhythm, captions and evidence placement. Superhuman informed compact action controls, without copying its atmospheric hero. The supplied plan takes precedence over skill suggestions for motion, exaggerated asymmetry and generic visual tricks.

## Reddit feedback applied

Role, context, output and validation appear together at the top. Navigation and chapter order are explicit. Hamza's name, contribution and first-person decisions replace product marketing. This establishes a repeatable case-study structure; it does not claim that the resume, homepage identity, or Locavore have already been standardized.

## Evidence boundaries

The source-of-truth inventory takes precedence over contradictory older copy. March and September artifacts remain distinct. Screenshots with placeholders are labelled. Visible controls do not prove completed behavior or usability. No fabricated interviews, metrics, quotes or timeline duration. Original model attribution remains unknown.
