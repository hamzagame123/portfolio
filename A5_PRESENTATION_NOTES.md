## 3. Case Study Walkthrough - Locavore (4.5 to 5.5 minutes)

### Project overview
Locavore is a mobile app concept for farmers markets and local food communities.
The main problem I focused on was that even though people want to shop local, the experience is fragmented.
Users have to search for markets, remember schedules, coordinate with friends, and keep track of vendors across different tools.

### My role
This was a solo UX/Product design project.
I worked on the problem framing, research synthesis, wireframes, interface design, and the high-fidelity prototype.

### Research and insights
One of the most important improvements I made was making the research easier to understand.
Through interviews and testing, I realized this was not just a discovery problem.
It was also a social coordination problem.
People wanted to know who was going, whether it was worth committing, and how to keep track of vendors across multiple markets.

### Design decisions
Based on that, I designed the product around a few core ideas:
- easier market discovery
- social proof through the "Who's Going" feature
- vendor continuity across markets
- reminders to reduce drop-off

One key decision was making commitment visible.
Instead of just showing market information, the app is designed to help users actually decide to attend.

### Outcome
The outcome was a 15-screen interactive prototype.
In early testing, users completed key tasks successfully at about 92 percent.
The biggest thing I learned was that trust and clarity matter as much as functionality.
